Score each ON ITS OWN MERITS first, independently of the other, then pick the
more creative one from the scores.

Score each 0-10 for overall creativity, judging against:
1. Originality — non-obvious, inventive approach.
2. Usefulness — actually addresses the brief.
3. Feasibility — physically and technically realistic.
4. Specificity — concrete mechanisms, not vague advice.
5. Elegance — solves with low added complexity, cost, or side effects.

Rules:
- Score using ONLY the content. A concept's score must be the SAME whether it is
  labelled A or B — the labels are random and mean nothing.
- Ignore position, order, length, formatting and writing style entirely. A longer
  concept is not a better concept.
- The more creative one is simply the one with the higher score.
- If and only if the scores are exactly equal, break the tie toward the more
  concrete and feasible mechanism — never by position.
